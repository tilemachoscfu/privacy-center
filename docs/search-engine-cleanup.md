# Search-engine cleanup

1. Remove or redact the information at the original website first. This is the durable fix.
2. After the source changes, request cache/outdated-content refresh from Google and Bing.
3. Use Google “Results about you” and Google personal-information removal forms for eligible contact, doxxing, identity or sensitive-data results.
4. Use Bing content-removal/outdated-cache requests for Bing results.
5. Record request URL, date, case number and recheck date in `data/data-brokers.csv` or a dedicated case note.

Removing a search result only suppresses the link in that search engine and jurisdiction; it does not remove the source page, copies, archives or other search indexes. Removing the source content eliminates the underlying publication, but cached/indexed copies may persist until refreshed.

Do not submit identity documents unless the official process requires them. Redact non-required fields and retain a local record of what was transmitted.
